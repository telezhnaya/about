# Who the hell is the DevRel

**TL;DR:** DevRel is a good friend of the developers — both outside and inside the company.

## **Why Engineers Need DevRel**

### **Shielding the engineering team from distractions**

Engineering time is precious. Engineers usually aren’t too happy about talking to the community. Unless you want to, let me gather all the information. I’ll give you all the context in a compressed and batched format. Keep working without worrying about external communication.

### **Being the translator between code and business**

DevRel shows others what we build, why it matters, and where it hurts — in plain language everyone understands. This speeds up all processes and makes them more transparent. As a result, engineers feel more satisfied because they see how their work affects the business.

### **Being the voice of the community**

Anything you want to know from our community — ask me. Either I know it, or I’ll find the answer.

### **Organizing knowledge-sharing sessions**

This helps employees grow as professionals, learn more about the project, and supports newcomers during onboarding.

## **How the Marketing Team Benefits from DevRel**

- Building a strong and supportive community
- Designing the experience external developers have with our product
- Being the technical expert for the external audience
- Providing high-quality learning materials: docs, videos, demos, code samples, etc.
- Helping with accuracy and authenticity in marketing messages
- Organizing events — both online and offline

All these points contribute to the product’s image, simplifying the attraction of new users and improving the satisfaction and retention of existing ones.

## **What DevRel Brings to the Company**

### **Finding patterns behind feedback, prioritizing and addressing real problems**

DevRel sits between Engineering, Users, Marketing, and C-level. This position gives a great opportunity to analyze high-level issues and find systematic ways to solve them.

### **Sharing the vision and mission inside and outside**

DevRel is responsible for many types of communication. All of this communication — even short chat messages — reflects our current vision and strategy. DevRel should represent the company’s values at all times.
